# Name and logo

"Yuva" and the Yuva logo identify the project maintained by productdevbook. The code licenses do
not grant rights to them.

You may:
- say that your product uses, integrates with or is built on Yuva;
- use the name, unchanged, to refer to the unmodified project;
- keep the name in a private, internal deployment, modified or not.

You may not, without written permission:
- offer a hosted or managed service, or a product, under the Yuva name or a confusingly similar one;
- publish a modified version under the Yuva name — rename your fork;
- use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement by the project.

Ask through a GitHub issue if you are unsure.
